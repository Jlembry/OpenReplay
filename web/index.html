About OpenReplay

Open a real CS2 .dem file to parse its rounds and player positions on this computer. Use the round list to jump, or scrub the full-match timeline. Toggle names and grid; maps with upper/lower radar textures default to stacked view. Choose Layered then select Upper or Lower without changing to stacked view.

Keyboard: Space plays/pauses; Left/Right seek 5 seconds; Up/Down jump rounds.

Events: Choose a kill to jump to its exact tick. Click scoreboard players to filter the map and event panel without changing the timeline. Use the separate Timeline players menu to follow individual events while keeping everyone visible. Zoom or pan the timeline with mouse wheel or the timeline controls. Kills reveal only when they happen. Use Types and the Utility Players multi-select to filter grenade types and throws involving several selected players without changing the radar player filter. Utility cards display damage and blinded players; click for a detailed per-player breakdown. Projectile paths use recorded samples, without invented endpoints. Smoke/fire boundaries and HE radii are illustrative, not game-engine simulations.

Map camera: Scroll to zoom, drag to pan, Shift + drag to select players in a rectangle without pausing, Ctrl (either key) + drag to draw map-anchored white annotations, and Backspace to erase all drawing. Use Show everyone to clear a player selection. Reset View resets only camera zoom/pan, not annotations.

Privacy: Your demo is processed only in this browser tab and is not uploaded to OpenReplay. Static website requests still reach the hosting provider.
